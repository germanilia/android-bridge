# Persona — Meeting Completion and Calendar Enrichment

## Owner-User

- **Context**: Uses Android Bridge personally on a Mac with one or more calendar accounts, commonly including Google Calendar.
- **Goals**: End recording quickly, continue working immediately, identify the correct calendar event, avoid repetitive customer entry, and choose when a note enters Second Brain.
- **Needs**: Local-first processing, minimal permissions, clear progress, recoverable failures, conservative customer suggestions, and manual control for ambiguity.
- **Frustrations**: Recording completion that appears frozen, automatic modal interruptions, duplicate notes, and incorrect customer assignment.
- **Technical comfort**: Comfortable configuring macOS Internet Accounts and reviewing generated meeting metadata, but expects normal actions to require little setup.

## Story Mapping

The Owner-User is the actor for every story in `meeting-calendar-enrichment-stories.md`.
